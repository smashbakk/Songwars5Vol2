# Prologue Scene Script — DRAFT

**Property of LiveLayer Technology LLC.**

Status: **DRAFT**, written by Muse on 2026-11-08 for the user's edits. Every story beat in it is already locked; the words are what's being drafted. **Exception: Smash's two lines in Scene 5 are the user's own words, given by voice note on 2026-11-08 — they are verbatim and final, not draft.**

**How to read this script:** The player picks their fighter at every match preview, so the player's side is written as reaction beats, not dialogue — the opponents carry the scenes, and the script plays identically for any pick. If the player picks the fighter they're about to face (mirror), the same scenes run unchanged: the mirror is a match rule, not a story event, and gets no fiction explanation. Scene format for the first playable: temporary illustrated panels with dialogue text (subtitles on by default). The full game replaces these with video assets using this same script. Soundtrack runs very quietly under every scene, per the locked presentation rules.

**Locked beats this script must hit:** the pull-in; the erasure stakes; SongWars5 as the road's end; fights in order Bank Sinatra → Rico Ruffino → Big Hood Tha King; the cause stays hidden (hints and misdirection only — the full reveal waits for the finale, through Smash); the closing tease in two beats — Smash's voice first, then his silhouette at the gate. No copies, no doubles, no body switching anywhere in this script.

## SCENE 1 — THE PULL-IN (opening, before Fight 1)

*Length: ~45 seconds. Skippable after the midpoint on repeat viewings. Panels: 4.*

**Panel 1.** Night. A phone screen, a poster, a stage light — fragments of the real world, mid-moment. The player's fighter is where they were a second ago. Then the sound bends: a crowd that isn't there, a bass note that doesn't stop.

**Panel 2.** Static crawls across everything like frost. Names — on the poster, on the screen — flicker. For one frame, the player's fighter's name is *gone*. Then it's back, wrong somehow. Thinner.

**Panel 3.** The world comes back as illustration: a rooftop over a city that almost looks right. The sky is too still. No wind. No birds. A bracket hangs in the sky like a scoreboard nobody built — ten names, lit. One of them is already dimmer than the rest.

**Panel 4.** Text, no speaker — the world's own voice, flat as a system message:

> **THE WORLD (text):** "You were making noise. Something heard it."
> **THE WORLD (text):** "Fight. Win your road to SongWars — November 22. Reach the end with your name still lit…"
> **THE WORLD (text):** "…or stop being worth remembering. No songs. No name. Nobody waiting for you at home — because nobody will know you were ever there."

*Note: the dim name in Panel 3 is never identified. It is the story's first hint that erasure is already happening to somebody.*

→ **FIGHT 1: vs BANK SINATRA** *(Bank's signature track takes over as the scene hands off to the loading screen.)*

## SCENE 2 — THE BUSINESSMAN (before Fight 1's start, at the match preview — plays once, before the player's first attempt)

*Length: ~30 seconds. Skippable after the set point. Panels: 3.*

Bank Sinatra is already there when the player arrives, unbothered, like he got pulled in mid-schedule and decided to charge it to the game.

> **BANK SINATRA:** "You look lost. Everybody's lost. Difference is, I read the room."
> **BANK SINATRA:** "This place runs on one currency — wins. You spend losses, you get… spent."
> **BANK SINATRA:** *(a glance at the sky-bracket)* "See the dim one? That's not a warning. That's a price tag."

*The player fighter's reaction panel: no words. Fists up.*

> **BANK SINATRA:** "Nothing personal. Business is business — and right now, you're overhead."

→ **FIGHT 1 begins.** *On a win: Bank Sinatra's fighter chapter unlocks (locked rule). Bank's parting line, win or lose, delivered on the results screen's story beat:*

> **BANK SINATRA:** "Win or lose… it keeps the change."

## SCENE 3 — THE WOLF SMELLS IT (between Fight 1 and Fight 2)

*Length: ~30 seconds. Skippable after the set point. Panels: 3.*

A new stretch of the city — same rooftop world, wronger: the lights hum at a pitch just under hearing. Rico Ruffino is pacing. Not waiting — *circling*.

> **RICO RUFFINO:** "You smell that? No? Course you don't. Prey never does."
> **RICO RUFFINO:** "I been fighting whatever it puts in front of me since the lights went funny. You know what I figured out?"
> **RICO RUFFINO:** "It ain't testing us. It's *sorting* us."

*Panel: the bracket in the sky pulses once. Somewhere far off, too far to place, something that might be a chain settles.*

> **RICO RUFFINO:** "Run, stand — don't matter to me. The pack eats either way, and today… I'm the pack."

→ **FIGHT 2: vs RICO RUFFINO.** *On a win: Rico Ruffino's fighter chapter unlocks. Rico's parting line:*

> **RICO RUFFINO:** "Heh. Wolf's gotta respect teeth. Go on — the big one's waiting, and he don't pace. He *sits*. Like it's a throne… or a post."

*Note: "or a post" is the prologue's prisoner-guard hint — one word, deniable, gone before it lands. Do not underline it in art or sound.*

## SCENE 4 — THE HEAVY DOOR (between Fight 2 and Fight 3)

*Length: ~30 seconds. Skippable after the set point. Panels: 3.*

Big Hood Tha King fills the frame the way a door fills a doorway. He is not circling, not selling anything. He looks at the player fighter the way weather looks at a roof.

> **BIG HOOD THA KING:** "Two down. You think that makes you ready for what's past me?"
> **BIG HOOD THA KING:** "Ain't nothing past me but the gate. And the gate got a keeper."
> **BIG HOOD THA KING:** *(beat)* "Everybody who got this far asked me the same thing — 'what's he like?' I told 'em: you'll hear him before you see him. And you'll wish you hadn't."

→ **FIGHT 3: vs BIG HOOD THA KING.** *On a win: Big Hood Tha King's fighter chapter unlocks. His parting line, quieter than anything before it:*

> **BIG HOOD THA KING:** "Go on, then. He's awake now. We all heard the chain."

## SCENE 5 — THE GATE (closing scene, after Fight 3)

*Length: ~60 seconds. This is the prologue's major scene: UNSKIPPABLE on first viewing. Panels: 5. The soundtrack drops to almost nothing.*

**Panel 1.** The rooftop ends at a gate that was never on any horizon before: too tall, too dark, lit from behind by a light with no source. The player's fighter stops walking on their own — the body decides before the mind does.

**Panel 2.** The chain sound again. Closer. A link at a time, like something shifting its weight. Then — **the voice. First beat of the tease: heard, unseen.** It is not loud. It doesn't need to be.

> **TYSHAWN SMASH (voice, unseen):** "Free or forgotten, brothers — that's the whole menu."

**Panel 3.** Hold on the gate. Nothing moves. The line hangs there — and it doesn't sound like a threat. It sounds like a man reading out his own sentence.

**Panel 4.** **Second beat of the tease: the silhouette.** The light behind the gate shifts, and there he is — the biggest shape in the game, seated at the gate like a post, chains running from him into the dark on both sides. The game does NOT explain the chains. In the prologue he reads as what the cause keeps at its door — its weapon, its guard. The truth (a prisoner, not a servant) is finale information and stays unplayed here.

**Panel 5.** The silhouette does not rise. It doesn't have to. One more line, level as a toll:

> **TYSHAWN SMASH:** "Win it all back — or join the quiet ones."

*Cut to black on the bracket: one more name, somewhere in the ten, a shade dimmer than it was. CHAPTER 1 stands open.*

**— END OF PROLOGUE —**

## Production notes

- **Smash's lines are the user's own words.** Both Scene 5 lines were given by the user in a voice note on 2026-11-08 (recording on file: Songwars.m4a) — "Free or forgotten, brothers — that's the whole menu" and "Win it all back — or join the quiet ones." They are verbatim locks: no later draft rewrites them, and the voice note is the reference take for the tease's voice beat.
- **Smash's two lines are the only Smash dialogue in the prologue.** He is heard once and seen once, both in Scene 5. No other scene names him — the other fighters say "the gate," "the keeper," "the big one."
- **The cause is never named, voiced, or shown** anywhere in this script. Its only traces: the flat system-text in Scene 1, the self-writing bracket, the chain sounds, the dimming names.
- **Erasure motif, visual only:** names flicker (Scene 1), one bracket name runs dim (Scene 1), one more dims at the end (Scene 5). Nobody in dialogue explains the dimming beyond Bank's "price tag" line.
- **First playable staging:** all prologue fights take place on Rooftop, the slice's one stage; the gate in Scene 5 is a backdrop element at the rooftop's edge, not a new stage.
- **Attempts/retry behavior (locked):** scenes play once per match, before the first attempt; retries go straight to the fight with an optional Replay Story button. Scene 5 plays at prologue completion regardless of which fighter the player used for Fight 3.
- **Unlock beats fired by this script's fights (locked):** beating Bank unlocks Bank's fighter chapter; beating Rico unlocks Rico's; beating Big Hood unlocks Big Hood's. Each win also pays the winning fighter 1 upgrade point.
